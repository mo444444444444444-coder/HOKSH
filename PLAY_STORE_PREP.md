## Google Play account-owned steps
1. The developer account must be verified in Play Console.
2. The app bundle must be signed through Play App Signing or an approved signing key.
3. Store listing, Data safety, content rating and required developer information must be completed.
4. If the Play account is a new personal account created after 13 Nov 2023, Google currently requires a closed test with at least 12 opted-in testers continuously for 14 days before production access.
5. Submit the production access request and then the signed AAB.

The repository cannot safely create or store the developer's private signing key, Google account credentials, legal identity, payment profile or tester accounts. Those remain under the developer's control.
